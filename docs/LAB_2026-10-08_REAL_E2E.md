## 3. What changed in the code

- **Workspace** (`lab_pick.py` default): the plate beside the board, edges
  projected through the calibration (x 0.12–0.41, y −0.09 to the board), now
  `(0.17, −0.075, 0.39, 0.11)`. The board itself is never a pick surface:
  perception re-finds it in every photo and ignores detections on it.
- **Surface drop** (`--surface-drop`, default 0.015 m, `lab_pick` and console):
  the plate sits 8 mm below the board; the operator lowered the grasp a further
  4 + 3 mm by eye. Applied off the board only. Before it, both early grasps
  closed on nothing (width 9.5 / 9.8 mm = empty-closed) while reporting
  `holding: true`.
- **Object heights** (`lab_objects.yaml`: tape 0.018, hat 0.04) now measured
  from the board plane, so the surface drop is not added to them.
- **Marker check** closes the empty jaws to 10 mm, not 0 (stall → red LED,
  "grip lost" risk).
- **Speed cap** defaults 100 % in `lab_pick.py` and `lab_console.py`; the
  pendant slider is the speed.
- `touch` reports and warns on the RG2's tilt, not the flange's.
- Dev container: ZED passthrough (video cgroup rule, `video` group, `/dev`
  bind), CPU torch, `transformers<5`, `pillow>=9.1` (apt's 9.0.1 breaks
  OWLv2's import), `numpy<2`, `ur7e-hf-cache` volume.

## 4. Gotchas learned

- `observe` must be taught over the new board (`/teach observe` in the
  console): `ready` is on the far side of the robot, and the corner-3 touch
  pose had the elbow flipped (+1.57 vs −1.70), so planning home from it swung
  3.27 rad and was refused (correctly).
- The script's RG2 width check rejects the fully open 110.6 mm reading; move
  the jaws once from the pendant toolbar.
- Don't activate the host `.venv` (Python 3.12) inside the container (3.10).
- Timing: OWLv2 ~8 s on CPU, PLAN 15–32 s (every segment, 4 jaw angles for
  round objects), motion = nominal × 100 / slider at `--joint-rate 0.09`.
- `lab_pick.py` segfaults at interpreter exit after `RESULT` (exit code 139);
  harmless to motion, wrong exit code.
- The ZED USB stream occasionally shows a corrupted green row.
